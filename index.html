Email notifications

The owner of the Google Sheet gets an email when someone logs in, and a short summary of what they did (collections saved, edited or deleted, settings changed) a few minutes later. “Clear all” is emailed straight away. Everything is also kept in the ActivityLog tab of the sheet.

One-time setup in Apps Script: 1) paste the latest Code.gs; 2) run __authMail once from the editor and accept the permissions (send email, triggers); 3) Deploy → Manage deployments → edit → New version. Emails go to the account that owns the script. Optional Script Properties: NOTIFY_EMAIL to send elsewhere, NOTIFY = off to pause.

Names come from the “Your name” box at login, so they identify who, but they are not proof — the password is shared.
